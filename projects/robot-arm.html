3DOF robot arm

A three-axis robot arm built around an ESP32 and controlled wirelessly over Bluetooth.

ESP32RoboticsEmbedded
Context
Personal project
Tools
ESP32, MG90S servos, Dabble, perfboard

The problem

A small three-axis arm that moves reliably on battery power and can be driven wirelessly.

What I did

  • Chose and wired MG90S servos for the three joints
  • Designed the power system: a LiPo battery with a boost converter
  • Laid out the electronics on perfboard
  • Wrote firmware with Dabble Bluetooth control and a passive buzzer for audio feedback
  • Built and refined it iteratively through hands-on testing in a makerspace

Results

  • A complete electronics and firmware package: servo drive, battery power, Bluetooth control, and buzzer feedback.

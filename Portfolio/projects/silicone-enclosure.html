Custom Silicone Sensor Enclosure

A client-based project focused on developing a custom silicone enclosure for a sensor used to collect human body movement data. The project involved translating the client's requirements into a manufacturable design through CAD modelling, 3D printing, and silicone casting.

Silicone CastingCAD3D Printing
Context
Makerspace | Client Project
Tools
SolidWorks, 3D Printing, Silicone Casting

The problem

The client required a custom silicone enclosure to house a sensor used for collecting human body movement data. The enclosure needed to accommodate the sensor's geometry while providing a suitable form factor for its intended application.

What I did

  • Collaborated directly with the client to understand the sensor's dimensions, intended application, and enclosure requirements.
  • Developed a custom mold design in CAD to produce the silicone enclosure.
  • Prepared and 3D printed the mold for fabrication.
  • Carried out the silicone casting process to manufacture the enclosure.
  • Worked through the design and fabrication process to translate the client's requirements into a physical prototype.

Results

  • Developed and fabricated a custom silicone sensor enclosure through an integrated design and manufacturing process. The project provided practical experience in client communication, mold design, additive manufacturing, and silicone casting, while demonstrating how digital designs can be translated into functional physical components.

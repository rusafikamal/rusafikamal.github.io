MORPH

An ESP32-Powered, Hackable Handheld Platform

ESP32EmbeddedHardware
Context
Personal project
Tools
ESP32, SolidWorks, ST7789 TFT Display, TP4056, DC-DC Buck Converter, Arduino IDE

What I did

  • Designed a custom enclosure to house the ESP32, TFT display, rotary encoder, buttons, and supporting electronics within a compact handheld form factor.
  • Developed an integrated rechargeable power system using a TP4056 charging module, DC-DC buck converter, and physical power switch.
  • Integrated the electronic components into a single enclosure, considering component placement, accessibility, and internal wiring.
  • Optimized display rendering to reduce flickering and improve responsiveness.

Results

  • MORPH has evolved into a functional handheld prototype that combines custom mechanical design, rechargeable power management, and interactive embedded software. The project has provided hands-on experience in integrating mechanical, electrical, and software systems into a compact device.

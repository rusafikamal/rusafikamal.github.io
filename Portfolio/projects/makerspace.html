Makerspace supervision and training

Running, maintaining, and documenting the fabrication equipment in the Faculty of Engineering Makerspace.

3D printingMaintenanceDocumentation
Context
uOttawa Faculty of Engineering
Status
Ongoing
Tools
Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra, Raised3D N2 Plus, IdeaMaker, laser cutters

The problem

Students need to use printers and cutters safely and get good results, and the equipment has to stay reliable.

What I did

  • Wrote wiki documentation for resin (MSLA) 3D printing on the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra, including safety procedures and workflow
  • Troubleshooting left nozzle flow on a Raised3D N2 Plus dual-extrusion printer with custom IdeaMaker start G-code
  • Maintain 3D printers, laser cutters, and other machinery
  • Train students on the equipment as a Makerspace Trainer and Bambu Lab Campus Ambassador

Results

  • A written resin printing procedure, including safety, for the Saturn 4 Ultra
  • Ongoing work on dual-extrusion nozzle flow

Gooseneck topology optimization

Using SolidWorks topology optimization to take weight out of a gooseneck part without losing the strength it needs.

Topology optimizationFEASolidWorks
Context
uOttawa Formula SAE
Status
In progress
Tools
SolidWorks Simulation

The problem

The gooseneck carries load, so any weight reduction has to leave it structurally sound.

What I did

  • Set up topology optimization in SolidWorks to suggest weight-reducing cutouts
  • Checking each candidate design with FEA to confirm it keeps its structural integrity

Formula SAE aerodynamics

Using data logger telemetry to find out why the car’s front wing and splitter kept touching the ground, then fixing it with a ride height change.

TelemetryAerodynamicsFormula SAE
Context
uOttawa Formula SAE, aerodynamics subteam
Status
Ongoing
Tools
AiM data logger, SolidWorks

The problem

The car’s front wing and splitter were making contact with the ground, and the team needed to know why.

What I did

  • Analyzed AiM data logger telemetry to find when the contact happened
  • Compared braking and acceleration phases to isolate the cause
  • Recommended a ride height change and helped implement it

Results

  • The contact was caused by braking-induced pitch, not acceleration
  • A 39 mm increase in static front ride height reduced incidents by about 80%
